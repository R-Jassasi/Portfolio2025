CAFINTO

Graphic Design | Branding

2017-2025

A roastery and cafe branding I designed while working with PixoPros - Muscat, Oman.

This project, executed with PixoPros in Oman during 2020, remains one of my favorites. My task was to rebrand a roastery and café in Muscat, giving it a more modern and minimalist touch. This involved redesigning the logo, selecting colors and fonts, developing a website, and designing packaging and coffee bags.
