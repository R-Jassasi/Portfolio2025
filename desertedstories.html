Deserted Stories

3D | Immersive Experience | AI

2025

Deserted Stories is an immersive 3D interactive experience designed to educate users about Oman’s 20th-century history through exploratory digital navigation and real-time conversational AI characters. The project merges digital storytelling, cultural representation, and spatial computing to reimagine how historical content can be delivered in public spaces like museums or digital exhibitions. It addresses a critical gap in immersive media by focusing on underrepresented Middle Eastern cultural narratives—specifically Oman’s social, political, and architectural landscape before the 1970s. Through iterative development, the project explored how digital storytelling, cultural fidelity, and AI technologies can work together to deepen user understanding of historical narratives. Conversations with AI characters are designed to encourage both learning and empathy, drawing from lived experience, ethnographic sources, and contextualised knowledge banks

Characters Creation

Characters were created using MetaHuman and powered by Convai, enabling customized AI personalities and knowledge banks for real-time conversations. Custom clothing for all characters was designed in Marvelous Designer and Blender, then imported and rigged onto the MetaHuman characters in Unreal Engine 5. Some characters can also execute actions on command; for example, the oud player in the market map performs a short oud piece through an animation triggered with the UE5 Sequencer.


Making the Maps

The maps used assets from Quixel and UE5 Marketplace, while some were created and modified in Blender. Their structures were inspired by descriptions in books and photographs of historical towns across Oman.
